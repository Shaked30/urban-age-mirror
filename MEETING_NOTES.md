### Business Model

**What Urban Age Does:**
Urban Age facilitates urban renewal projects for residential buildings through a resident-friendly process.

**The Process:**
1. **Lead Generation:** Residents visit site and leave their details
2. **Viability Check:** Urban Age assesses if the building qualifies for renewal
3. **Partnership:** Urban Age finds a large construction company to execute the project
4. **Execution:** Partner construction company handles the actual renewal work

**Urban Age's Role:**
- Middle-man/facilitator between residents and construction companies
- Viability assessment specialists
- Project coordination
- Resident experience management

## 🚨 Key Risks & Considerations

### Risks Identified

**1. Dual Audience Challenge**
- Need to appeal to both residents (B2C) and construction companies (B2B)
- Different messaging needs
- Different credibility signals
- **Mitigation:** Resident-first homepage, dedicated partner/about section

**2. Educational Burden**
- Many residents don't understand TAMA/urban renewal
- Complex topic to explain simply
- Risk of overwhelming or confusing
- **Mitigation:** Visual process explanation, simple language, FAQ, one step at a time

**3. Trust Barrier**
- New company (potentially)
- Large decision for residents (their home)
- Requires significant trust
- **Mitigation:** Transparency, team visibility, clear process, testimonials if available

**4. Form Friction**
- Getting contact details is primary goal
- Too many fields = lower conversion
- Too few fields = less qualified leads
- **Mitigation:** A/B test, progressive disclosure, make it feel easy

### Considerations

**Hebrew/RTL Design:**
- Primary language is Hebrew (right-to-left)
- Design must work perfectly in RTL
- Font choices critical
- Testing in Hebrew essential

**Mobile-First:**
- Many users will be on mobile
- Forms must be mobile-friendly
- Click-to-call essential
- WhatsApp integration important

**Loading Speed:**
- Israeli internet infrastructure varies
- Fast loading critical for conversion
- Image optimization essential
- Minimal heavy scripts
