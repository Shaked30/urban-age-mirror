## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

During the meeting, be alert for:
- ❌ Extremely tight deadline with lots of work
- ❌ Very limited budget for scope
- ❌ Unclear or changing requirements
- ❌ Missing content with no plan to create
- ❌ Complicated Base44 situation
- ❌ Unrealistic expectations
- ❌ Too many decision-makers

## 📞 Communication Protocol

### During Meeting:
- Listen actively
- Take detailed notes
- Ask clarifying questions
- Don't overpromise
- Be realistic about timelines
- Show ONYX site for reference

### After Meeting:
- Send summary email promptly
- Set expectations clearly
- Communicate progress regularly
- Flag any blockers immediately
- Keep timeline commitments

## 💡 Tips for Success

### DO:
- ✅ Be professional and confident
- ✅ Show expertise but listen more than talk
- ✅ Get specific about requirements

- ✅ Get everything in writing
