- Payment terms to be discussed

- Offer options at different price points
- Be transparent about what's included

## 🚨 Red Flags & How to Handle

### Red Flag: "We need it live in 2 weeks"
**Response:** "Let me assess the current state first. I'll provide realistic timeline options based on what needs to be done. We can discuss phased approach if needed."

### Red Flag: "Budget is very limited"
**Response:** "I understand. After assessment, I'll present options including an MVP approach focusing on must-haves first, with enhancements in phase 2."

### Red Flag: "We're not sure what we want yet"
**Response:** "No problem. Part of my process is helping clarify requirements. We'll use ONYX and other examples to define what works for your business."

### Red Flag: "Base44 was terrible"
**Response:** "I understand your frustration. Let me assess their work objectively and we'll determine the best path forward - whether continuing or rebuilding."

### Red Flag: "Can you also do X, Y, Z?" (scope creep starting)
**Response:** "Absolutely we can discuss that. Let me capture it as a 'nice-to-have' and we'll prioritize against must-haves and timeline."
