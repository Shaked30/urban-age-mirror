## 🎯 Quick Summary

**Urban Age** connects building residents with construction companies for urban renewal projects:
- Residents submit building details via website
- Urban Age checks if building qualifies for TAMA/renewal
- Urban Age finds construction partner
- Partner executes the renewal project

**Website Goal:** Lead generation (get residents to submit their info)

**Design Direction:** Friendly + Professional balance
- Simple and clear
- Warm and approachable
- Trustworthy and credible
- Warmer than typical corporate real estate sites

## 🎨 Key Design Principles

**Brand Values:**
- Simplicity
- Clarity
- Friendliness
- Professionalism
- Resident-First

**Visual Style:**
- Warm colors (blues/teals + coral/orange)
- Clean, modern sans-serif fonts (Hebrew support)
- Generous whitespace
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Real photos (Israeli buildings, people, team)

**Tone of Voice:**
- Warm but competent
- Helpful but professional
- Clear and simple (no jargon)
- Approachable and friendly
