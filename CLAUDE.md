# Claude Context: Urban Age Website

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2025

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

**Client:** Tal, Sagi, Gilad (friends)
**Company:** Urban Age
**Business:** Urban renewal facilitation (TAMA) in Israel

### Business Model
1. Building residents visit website and submit their details
2. Urban Age assesses if building qualifies for TAMA/urban renewal
3. Urban Age finds a large construction company partner
4. Construction company executes the renewal project

### Urban Age's Role
- Facilitator/middleman between residents and construction companies
- Viability assessment specialists
- Make the process simple and clear for residents

---

## 🎯 Website Goals

### Primary Goal
**Lead Generation:** Get building residents to submit their contact information

### Secondary Goal
**Credibility:** Appear professional and trustworthy to construction company partners

### Target Feeling
Residents should feel:
- "This will be simple and easy"
- "The process is clear"
- "These people are friendly and helpful"
- "These people are professional and trustworthy"
- "My experience will be smooth"

---

## 🎨 Brand & Design Direction

### Brand Values
- **Simplicity** - Making complex processes easy
- **Clarity** - Clear communication, no confusion
- **Friendliness** - Warm and approachable
- **Professionalism** - Competent and reliable
- **Resident-First** - Caring about homeowner experience

### Design Direction
**Balance:** Friendly + Professional
- Warmer and more approachable than ONYX Group (reference site)
- Still credible and trustworthy
- NOT too corporate/stuffy
- NOT too casual/unprofessional
- Clean, simple, uncluttered
- Clear visual hierarchy

### Visual Style
**Colors:** Warm blues/teals + coral/orange accents, greens
**Fonts:** Hebrew-friendly (Heebo, Assistant, Rubik)
**Imagery:** Real Israeli buildings, happy people, authentic team photos
**Layout:** Clean, generous whitespace, obvious CTAs, mobile-first

### Tone of Voice
✅ "We're here to make urban renewal simple and stress-free for you"
✅ "Let's see if your building qualifies - it only takes a minute"
✅ "We handle the complexity so you don't have to"

❌ "Urban Age facilitates strategic real estate renewal initiatives"
❌ "Hey there! Want to upgrade your building?"
