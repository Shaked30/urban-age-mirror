## 🎯 Business Model (30-Second Summary)

Urban Age connects building residents with construction companies for urban renewal projects:

1. **Residents** visit site and submit their building details
2. **Urban Age** assesses if building qualifies for renewal
3. **Urban Age** finds a construction partner to execute
4. **Construction company** handles the actual renewal work

**Urban Age's Role:** Facilitator and viability specialist who makes the process easy for residents

## 🎨 Design Requirements

### Visual Style

**Color Palette:**
- Warm and friendly (not corporate blue/gray)
- Professional (not playful/childish)
- Approachable (not intimidating)
- Suggestions: Warm blues, teals, coral/orange accents, greens

**Typography:**
- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Excellent Hebrew support (RTL)
- High readability
- Friendly but professional
- Suggestions: Heebo, Assistant, Rubik

**Imagery:**
- Real buildings and people (Israeli context)
- Happy residents/homeowners
- Professional but warm
- Team photos (authentic, approachable)
- Before/after building transformations
- Avoid generic stock photos

**Layout:**
- Clean and uncluttered
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Obvious CTAs
- Easy to scan
- Mobile-first

### Tone of Voice Examples

**✅ Good (Friendly + Professional):**
- "We're here to make urban renewal simple and stress-free for you"
- "Let's see if your building qualifies - it only takes a minute"
- "We handle the complexity so you don't have to"

**❌ Avoid (Too Corporate):**
- "Urban Age facilitates strategic real estate renewal initiatives"
- "Leveraging our extensive network of construction partners"

**❌ Avoid (Too Casual):**
- "Hey there! Want to upgrade your building?"
- "Hit us up and we'll make magic happen"

## 🚨 Key Considerations

### Critical Factors

**1. Dual Audience Balance**
- Homepage: Resident-first (lead generation priority)
- About/Partner section: Construction company credibility
- Messaging must work for both

**2. Educational Challenge**
- TAMA/urban renewal is complex
- Must explain simply
- Visual aids critical
- One step at a time approach

**3. Trust Building**
- Large decision for residents (their home)
- Transparency essential
- Team visibility important
- Clear process reduces anxiety

**4. Hebrew/RTL Design**
- Primary language Hebrew (right-to-left)
- Font choice critical
- All design must work in RTL
- Test thoroughly in Hebrew

**5. Mobile-First**
- Many users on mobile
- Forms must be mobile-perfect
- Click-to-call essential
- WhatsApp integration important

**6. Form Optimization**
- Primary conversion goal
- Balance: enough fields vs. friction
- Make it feel easy and quick
- Clear value proposition
