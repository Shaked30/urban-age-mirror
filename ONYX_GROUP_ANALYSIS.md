# Reference Website Analysis: ONYX Group (onyx-group.co.il)

**Analysis Date:** November 23, 2025
**Website:** https://onyx-group.co.il/
**Purpose:** Primary reference for Tal Dayan's website project

---

## Executive Summary

ONYX Group operates a high-quality, professionally designed website for their luxury real estate and urban renewal business. This site represents the type of polished, trust-building digital presence appropriate for premium real estate development companies in Israel. The analysis below informs our approach for Tal Dayan's project.

---

## Business Overview

### Company Profile
**ONYX Group** - Leading Israeli real estate development company

**Core Business:**
- Luxury residential property development
- Urban renewal projects (Tama 38 focus)

- Full-service development (planning, execution, financing)

**Unique Positioning:**
- Partnership between Aget Engineering and Barak Real Estate
- "Masters of Quiet Luxury" brand positioning
- "Built to Inspire" tagline

- Focus on demand-driven central locations

**Target Market:**
- Affluent property investors
- Luxury homebuyers
- Urban renewal participants
- Major Israeli city residents (Tel Aviv, etc.)
